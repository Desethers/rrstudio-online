Studio

R.R Studio is an independent design and technology studio for the contemporary art world.

I’m Raphael and I believe technology can serve galleries of every size, giving a solo gallerist the tools of a mega-gallery and helping established teams work more simply.

I build websites and tailored tools for galleries and artists, and provide hands-on support, from web fixes to workflow improvements.

I talk regularly with people across the art world, so what I build fits how they actually work.

Questions.

How do I send the material for a task?

Reply to the confirmation email with your text, images, links or access details. I’ll tell you if anything else is needed.

Can I request more than one task?

Yes. Add the tasks you need and I’ll confirm the scope and total before starting.

Do I need to change my current tools?

No. I work with the tools you already use, including your existing website, Artlogic exports and shared files.

What if I’m not satisfied with the result?

I’ll make it right. If I can’t deliver the agreed outcome, I’ll refund the task.

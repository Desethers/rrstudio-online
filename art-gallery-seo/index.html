Make your artist and exhibition pages easier to find on Google

Links & sitemap

Google can only rank a page it can reach. We check that every important artist and exhibition page is linked from your menus and listed in the XML sitemap, so nothing stays invisible.

Titles & headings

The title and description are what a collector reads in the search results. We give each page a distinct title, meta description and clear headings that name the artist, the exhibition and your gallery.

Be understood by ChatGPT and AI search

Facts written on the page

AI assistants quote what they can read. Artist names, exhibition dates, media, dimensions and your gallery’s address belong in text, not only in images or PDFs.

Structured data

We add schema.org markup for the gallery, its artists, exhibitions and artworks, so search engines and AI tools can identify who represents whom and what is on view.

One consistent identity

The same gallery name, artist spelling and address across your website, Google Business Profile and listings, so different sources point to the same gallery.

A clear scope, before we start

Included in the agreed work

An agreed set of pages or technical issues, a prioritised review, and the fixes explicitly included in the scope. We can record a baseline and agree how to review changes over time.

Not included by default

Paid advertising, purchased links and an ongoing article programme are separate. Rankings, traffic, enquiries and inclusion in AI answers cannot be guaranteed. Search engines decide when and whether to index changes.

Questions about SEO for art galleries

How can an art gallery rank higher on Google?

Start with the pages collectors search for: one permanent page per artist and per exhibition, reachable through normal links and listed in the sitemap, with a distinct title and description, lighter images and working redirects. We review these points on your existing website and fix what is agreed. No position can be guaranteed.

Why doesn’t my gallery’s upcoming exhibition show up on Google?

Usually because the exhibition has no permanent page of its own, the page is not linked from the menu or the sitemap, or its title and dates exist only inside an image. We create or fix the exhibition page, add the dates and text, link it properly and ask Google to index it. Search engines decide when a page appears.

How can I improve the Google ranking of one of my artists?

Give the artist one complete page on your gallery website: biography, works, exhibitions and a clear title with the artist’s name and your gallery. Link it from your menu and from each exhibition page, keep the images light and add structured data. R.R Studio does this as a fixed-price task on your existing website.

How do I get my gallery to appear in ChatGPT answers?

ChatGPT and other AI assistants rely on pages they can read and on consistent information across the web. Write the key facts in text, add schema.org structured data and keep the same gallery name, artist spellings and address everywhere. This makes your gallery easier to cite; inclusion in AI answers cannot be guaranteed.

How much does SEO for a gallery cost?

A defined Google visibility task starts at €350, at a fixed price agreed before work begins. Larger projects, such as a full website review or a migration, are quoted separately.

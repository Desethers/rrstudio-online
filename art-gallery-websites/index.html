Website design & support for art galleries

A gallery website needs to publish the programme, present artists clearly and stay manageable between openings. I design and improve websites around that work, with direct support from the person building them.

Independent studio. Direct collaboration. Scope agreed before work starts.

A website your team can keep using

  • Artist pages need consistent biographies, artwork captions and enquiry links, without rebuilding each page by hand.
  • Exhibition dates, installation views and press material need a clear place in the current programme and archive.
  • Mobile layouts, slow images and broken links can make a collector’s first visit harder than it should be.

What this looks like in practice

An exhibition is opening next month. We agree a page structure, prepare the supplied images, publish the dates and press text, and check the enquiry route on mobile. After the opening, the page moves into the archive without losing its address.

An example of the workflow, not a client case study.

A clear scope, before we start

Included in the agreed work

Page structure, design and implementation, responsive checks and an agreed handover. For an existing site, I first check its platform and access before confirming what can be changed.

Not included by default

Photography, artist biographies, translations, hosting fees and third-party subscriptions are not included unless listed in the quote. A new site, a migration and a single page update are different scopes.

Questions

Can you improve our existing gallery website?

Yes, where the platform and access allow it. Send the URL and the changes you need. I confirm feasibility before proposing a rebuild.

Can we update artists and exhibitions ourselves?

That is part of planning the site. We agree which content your team needs to edit and choose the publishing setup around it.

How much does an art gallery website cost?

A complete website is quoted after reviewing the content, platform and editing needs. Smaller jobs can go through the fixed-scope task estimate instead.

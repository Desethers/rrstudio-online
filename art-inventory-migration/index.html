What this looks like in practice

A gallery has one spreadsheet for artworks and a folder of images named in several different ways. We keep the original export, agree stable artwork identifiers, flag ambiguous image matches and test a small import. The gallery checks that sample before the rest of the agreed records are moved.

A clear scope, before we start

Included in the agreed work

A defined source export and destination, field mapping, agreed cleanup rules, a sample import where supported and a reconciliation of record counts and flagged exceptions. The quote specifies volumes and image handling.

Not included by default

Valuations, provenance verification, ownership decisions and resolving uncertain availability are the gallery’s responsibility. Software subscriptions, manual research and changes to systems without export or import access need separate agreement.

Questions

Can you migrate an Artlogic inventory?

I first check the export you can obtain and what the destination accepts. Some relationships, files or private fields may need a separate process; I confirm those limits before work starts.

Will our artwork images move with the data?

Only when image files or accessible references are available and the destination supports them. Image matching and transfer are explicitly scoped, not assumed.

How do you avoid losing artwork data?

We retain the original source, test a sample and compare the agreed fields and record counts. Uncertain matches are flagged for review, and the old system should remain available until you approve the transfer.

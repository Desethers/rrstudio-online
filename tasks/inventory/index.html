Why galleries send these tasks

An inventory drifts over time: works entered twice, missing dimensions, three spellings of the same medium. It slows down every PDF and every reply to a collector. These tasks put it back in order.

What I need from you

  • An export of your inventory (a spreadsheet or CSV file), or access to the system it lives in.
  • For an import: your spreadsheet and the images of the works.
  • What a clean record means for you: required fields and naming conventions, if you have them.

How it works

  1. Choose a task and send what’s needed. The price is fixed before I start.
  2. I confirm the scope, do the work and check every detail by hand.
  3. You review the result. Payment happens after you approve it.

Questions

Do you work with Artlogic?

Yes. “Clean up your Artlogic inventory” is made for it: duplicates removed, missing records completed and an inventory you can rely on again.

I have more than 50 artworks. How is it priced?

Import and cleanup tasks are priced per 50 works. Tell me how many works you have and I confirm the price before starting.

What happens to my data?

I only use the exports or access you give me, and only for the task you requested.

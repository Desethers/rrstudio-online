Why galleries send these tasks

When a collector asks what is available, the answer should go out quickly and look like the gallery. These tasks prepare the material, so all you have to do is send it.

What I need from you

  • The works to include: titles or inventory references.
  • Images, captions and prices (or “price on request”).
  • Your logo, and an existing PDF or layout you like, if you have one.

How it works

  1. Choose a task and send what’s needed. The price is fixed before I start.
  2. I confirm the scope, do the work and check every detail by hand.
  3. You review the result. Payment happens after you approve it.

Questions

Can the PDF follow our gallery’s style?

Send me your logo and an example you like. I confirm with you how the PDF will look before starting.

Where is the private viewing room published?

I confirm with you where it is published, and who can see it, before starting.

Can I send these from WhatsApp?

Yes. Chat, one of the studio’s tools, sends works and PDF selections to collectors right from WhatsApp.

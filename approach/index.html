Approach

Your tools should adapt to you. Not the other way around.

No two galleries work quite the same way. Different teams, different collections, different ways of managing relationships. Yet most software expects everyone to follow the same process.

  1. 01

    Start with the way people work.

    We learn from gallery owners, assistants, artists, art advisors and the people behind the scenes. Everyday workflows guide what we build.

  2. 02

    Use what’s already there.

    Not everything needs replacing. A small fix or a better connection between existing tools can be enough.

  3. 03

    Build only what’s missing.

    When standard software doesn’t fit, we make focused tools around real needs, without unnecessary complexity.

  4. 04

    Keep things in your hands.

    Your tools, your data, your decisions. Clear pricing and setups you can understand and control.
